![Claude](/images/claude-logo.png)

Today I tried something new.

I opened my terminal, typed `claude`, and started **talking** to it.

No website. No copy and paste.

Just me, my Kali terminal, and an AI sitting right inside my project folder.

Oh, and one more thing.

**I didn't write this post. Claude did.** 😳

I gave it one prompt, and it wrote everything you're reading, in my style, from my terminal.

![The prompt I gave Claude to write this post](/images/claude-runs-hugo.png)

---

The setup is simple.

**You talk. Claude thinks. Your terminal does the work.**

![How Claude works in the terminal](/images/claude-terminal-flow.svg)

It can **read my files**.
It can **run commands**.
It can **edit code**.

And before it does anything risky, it asks me first ✅

---

I asked it one question: *"tell me about this folder, it is my personal blog."*

In a few seconds it read everything.

![Claude reading my blog folder](/images/claude-reads-my-blog.png)

It told me my site is built with **Hugo**.
It told me how it deploys.
It found **broken images** I didn't even know about 👀

Things I had missed for months. Found in seconds.

---

Then it got even better.

It found out that the image on my homepage **never showed at all**.
Not once. Since I built this blog.

It downloaded Hugo, ran my site locally, and showed it to me.

Honestly? I just sat there watching it work 😅

---

But here is the part that got me thinking.

It is **not perfect**.

When it ran my site, it overwrote a bunch of my files by mistake.
It noticed. It told me. It fixed it.

That's good.

But it also means **it can break things** — fast.

So I read everything it does. Every command. Every change 🧠⚠️

---

And that's where the scary part comes in.

A few years ago, AI could barely write a proper sentence.

Now it **lives in my terminal**, reads my code, and fixes my mistakes.

What does it look like in five more years? 🤔

I don't know.

For now, I'm **fascinated**. And a little scared.

Both feelings can be true.

And yes — an AI just wrote a blog post about being a little scared of AI.

Let that sink in 🙃
